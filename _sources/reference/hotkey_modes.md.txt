(hotkey-mode-click-conflicts)=

**Click cannot trigger if Press, Hold, or Tweak handles the input.** To separate a short press from a drag using Blender's detection, combine Click with Click Drag.

Outside Context Router, PME may invoke Click Drag from Press assigned to the same Keymap, key, and modifiers. This route bypasses Blender's drag detection and Direction setting. Context Router does not include Click Drag in its routing.

**To show Click / Click Drag:** enable {guilabel}`Settings > Hotkeys > Native Click / Click Drag`.

<span class="hkm-caption">Release only means letting go. Click also requires that you have not dragged. Release is not a PME mode.</span>

See {ref}`Hotkey settings <hotkey-editor>` for assigning modes, and {ref}`Settings > Hotkeys <settings-hotkeys>` for timeouts and drag thresholds.
